U.S. Patent No. 9,123,456 (Fictional Reference)

Title: Method for Ranking Digital Documents

Abstract:
A system for ranking digital documents based on relevance scoring. The system assigns weights to documents using similarity metrics and usage statistics.

Column 3, Lines 10–45:

The system receives multiple documents and computes a relevance score for each document based on similarity to a query vector. Documents are then ranked and displayed in order of relevance.

The relevance score may include factors such as:
- textual similarity between query and document content
- frequency of document access
- timestamp of last modification

The ranked list is updated when a user interacts with a document, including clicks or time spent viewing the document.
