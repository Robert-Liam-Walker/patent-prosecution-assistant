U.S. Patent No. 8,765,432

Title: System for Prioritizing Legal Documents Using Citation Networks

Abstract:
A system for ranking legal documents based on citation relationships and usage frequency.

Column 2, Lines 15–55:

Legal documents within a corpus are assigned importance scores based on citation frequency within a legal database.

Documents that are frequently cited by other legal documents receive higher priority scores.

The system may adjust rankings based on user interaction history and document popularity over time.

A ranked list of legal documents is generated and displayed to a user interface.

The system may combine citation-based scoring with similarity-based ranking techniques known in the art.
