Office Action Summary

Application No.: 17/555,111

Rejection:

Claim 1 is rejected under:
- 35 U.S.C. §102 as anticipated by U.S. Patent No. 9,123,456
- 35 U.S.C. §103 as obvious over U.S. Patent No. 9,123,456 in view of U.S. Patent No. 8,765,432

Examiner Findings:

US 9,123,456 discloses:
- receiving legal documents
- generating relevance scores based on similarity and usage metrics
- ranking documents in a list
- displaying ranked results
- updating rankings based on user interaction

US 8,765,432 discloses:
- citation-based scoring of legal documents
- ranking based on citation frequency within a legal corpus
- combining citation metrics with similarity-based ranking techniques

The combination renders Claim 1 obvious under §103.
