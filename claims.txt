U.S. Patent Application No. 17/123,456 (Fictional Test Case)

Title: System for Adaptive Legal Document Prioritization

CLAIM 1:

A computer-implemented method comprising:

(a) receiving a plurality of legal documents associated with a case file;

(b) generating a relevance score for each legal document based on:
    (i) semantic similarity to a user query,
    (ii) recency of the legal document, and
    (iii) citation frequency within a legal corpus;

(c) ranking the plurality of legal documents based on the relevance score;

(d) displaying a ranked list of legal documents to a user interface;

(e) updating the relevance score dynamically in response to user interaction with the documents.
