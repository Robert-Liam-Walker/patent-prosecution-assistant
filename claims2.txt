U.S. Patent Application No. 17/555,111 (Test Case 1)

Title: System for Adaptive Legal Document Ranking with Dynamic Reweighting

CLAIM 1:

A computer-implemented method comprising:

(a) receiving a plurality of legal documents associated with a case file;

(b) generating a relevance score for each legal document based on:
    (i) semantic similarity between a user query and content of each legal document,
    (ii) recency of each legal document within the case file, and
    (iii) citation frequency of each legal document within a legal corpus;

(c) ranking the plurality of legal documents based on the relevance scores;

(d) displaying a ranked list of legal documents in a graphical user interface;

(e) updating the relevance scores dynamically based on user interaction with the displayed documents;

wherein the relevance score is recalculated after each user interaction event.
