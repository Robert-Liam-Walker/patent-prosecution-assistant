Office Action Summary

Application No.: 17/123,456

Rejection: Claim 1 is rejected under 35 U.S.C. §102 as being anticipated by U.S. Patent No. 9,123,456.

Examiner Findings:

Reference 9,123,456 discloses:
- receiving multiple legal or digital documents
- generating relevance scores based on similarity and usage metrics
- ranking documents according to computed scores
- displaying ranked results to a user interface
- updating rankings based on user interaction (clicks, engagement time)

Therefore, all limitations of Claim 1 are disclosed in the prior art reference.
